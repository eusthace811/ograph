ograph-cli are unchanged since ee48efb, the binary agent-rca-haiku ran):
  replay_calls.py --runs docs/evidence/agent-rca-haiku --bin target/release/ograph
    --cases <data>/rcaeval-cases --demo <main checkout>/examples/demo --wrong-only
    --work /private/tmp/claude-501/lane-u-work/replay --out replay-wrong.jsonl
  misled_by.py --replayed replay-wrong.jsonl --labels docs/evidence/misled-by-labels.txt --suite rcaeval
  misled_by.py --replayed replay-wrong.jsonl --suite rcaeval-miss   (and --suite demo)

Why a replay: the transcripts keep 3000 characters of each output, and the wrong application is
usually past the cut (ograph_before and ograph_chains answers run 4-9 KB). replay_calls.py
re-executes every call of the 123 wrong runs against the same staged case and checks each output
against the kept text: 996 of 1073 calls match (932 exact, 64 close), 62 do not (44 are `ls`, `find`
and `which`, whose paths and dates differ in the replay; 9 the agent's own Python), and 15
were previews the agent saw in place of a large output. Where a replay does not match, the kept
text stands in.
