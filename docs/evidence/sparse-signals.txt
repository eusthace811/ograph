Lane W: sparse signals. Tell the agent, in ograph's own outputs, when an event's strength rests on
a baseline too sparse to measure spread.

GATE, written and committed before any code of this lane (2026-11-25)
=====================================================================
Problem (diagnosed on dev data only: the distractor suite at seed 2026, the RCAEval seen sample
and the demo). The metric detector scores a point by robust z against a trailing baseline with a
scale of max(1.4826 MAD, 1% of |median|, range/6, 1e-6). A series that is zero at almost every
baseline point (a sparse series) has MAD 0, median 0 and a range of at most a few spikes, so the
scale falls to the 1e-6 floor or to a spread set by a handful of points, and any change saturates
strength at 1.0. On the seed-2026 suite every sparse distractor event has 29 baseline points, all
zero, and severity 1.00; the mcp arm (lane T's build) answers it on 19 of 21 runs. On RCAEval the
same shape is redis's disk series (51 of its strong events have an all-zero baseline). Lane T's
`chronic` does not catch a sparse series that changes once.

Change (planned): the metric detector records, on each episode whose baseline is sparse (median
zero and fewer than 12 nonzero points, 12 being the points the detector already requires for a
baseline), how many of its baseline points were nonzero, as a new event fact in the ontology.
`before`, `chains` (root and links), `why`, the MCP tools and the lab mark such events `sparse`,
with a sentence that says the strength is unreliable, as lane T's `chronic` is shown. No service
name, nothing suite- or RCAEval-specific; the threshold is the detector's own baseline minimum.
The first candidate leaves chain scoring unchanged. A variant whose scoring uses a corrected
strength for sparse events may be tried on dev data only; it is kept only if D1 holds with no
flip and chains rank 1 on the seed-2026 suite does not drop, and then agent check 4 applies.

median zero and N < 12 nonzero points. Such an event carries `sparse`: "media-meta-db
snapshot-io was zero at every point of its baseline: any change scores full strength, so its
strength is unreliable; without other events on media-meta-db, this one is not evidence of a
fault". Shown in `before`, on chain root and links in `chains`, in `why`, the lab, and the MCP
tool descriptions; skills/ograph gets three sentences. Scores and ranks are not touched, so A4
does not apply.
